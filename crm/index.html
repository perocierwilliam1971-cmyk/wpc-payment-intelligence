How the colors work

New just added, no real engagement yet.

Warm engaged: replied to you, or ran a statement analysis on the website. Leads move here automatically when they reply.

Sale closed. Any running sequence stops.

Opens never change the color on their own: Apple Mail and corporate security scanners "open" emails automatically, so an open alone isn't proof of interest. Opens logged within 2 minutes of sending are shown faded as "possibly automatic".
