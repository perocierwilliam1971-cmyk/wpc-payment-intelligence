Honest answers

Will this get me an interview?

No tool can promise that. CareerLift helps you present your real experience clearly and professionally; interviews depend on the job market, your fit and many other factors.

Is it “ATS-approved”?

There is no official ATS approval. CareerLift creates PDFs with real, selectable text and standard headings, and includes a plain-text version and an ATS checklist. The Classic ATS template is the safest choice for large online portals.

Does it use AI?

No. Achievement Detective™ Lite uses structured writing rules and your own answers. It never invents accomplishments, employers, numbers or qualifications.

Where is my information stored?

In your own browser on your device. You can download a backup file anytime. Clearing your browser data, private browsing, or long periods of inactivity (Safari may clear site data after 7 days without visits) can erase it — keep a backup.

Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. It is designed for Safari and Chrome on iPhone and iPad, plus Android, Windows and Mac. On iOS, use “Share / Save to Files” to store your documents.
